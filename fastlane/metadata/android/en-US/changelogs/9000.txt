Keep stickers on the phone: keep a sticker, a pack or the whole library offline from its menu, the pack screen or Settings, Available offline. Kept stickers insert and share with the server away; the others are dimmed. Downloads run on Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data, within a space limit you set. Thumbnails are kept by default; recently sent and liked stickers can be kept too. Sizes are shown in KiB and MiB.
